OUR PURPOSE

About HeightCompare

HeightCompare is a simple online utility designed to help people compare two or more heights visually. Enter heights in centimeters, meters, or feet and inches to see a proportional illustration and the numerical difference.

Designed to be simple

The tool works in a modern web browser and does not require an account. In this version, entered names and heights are processed locally in your browser rather than submitted to a website server.

Accuracy and limitations

The calculations use the height values entered by the visitor. The human figures are simplified illustrations and do not represent exact body proportions, posture, footwear, or measurement conditions. The tool is for general information and entertainment, not medical or professional measurement.

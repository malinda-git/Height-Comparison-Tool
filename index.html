How does height comparison work?

Enter each person's height and the tool converts the value to centimeters for a consistent comparison. The tallest person sets the visual scale, while each other silhouette is drawn proportionally. The difference between two people is calculated from their entered heights.

Height conversion guide

One foot equals 12 inches, and one inch equals 2.54 centimeters. To convert feet and inches to centimeters, multiply the total inches by 2.54. This tool performs the conversion automatically.

Is my information saved?

No account is needed. This version calculates heights in your browser and does not send the names or heights you enter to a server. If you refresh the page, your comparison may reset.
